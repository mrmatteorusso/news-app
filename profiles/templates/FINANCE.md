# Finance and markets profile

Prioritise developments with broad consequences for long-term investors, households, businesses, public finances, or financial stability.

High-interest subjects include central-bank decisions, inflation or labour data that materially change the outlook, recession/growth turning points, banking problems, sovereign debt, large fiscal packages, taxation, tariffs, regulation, extraordinary market movements, and systemically important corporate events.

Give explicit attention to final or highly consequential budgets from the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, and Italy. Omit minor amendments and routine market commentary.

Market arithmetic, highest-close comparisons, timestamps, and threshold checks are performed by PHP—not by the model.
