# AI, technology, and business profile

Select major capability, access, pricing, platform, agent, coding, computer-use, multimodal, API, open-source, and Chinese-model developments. Reject minor benchmarks and repetitive releases.

Give additional weight to evidence that shows where AI is actually creating or destroying value:

- genuine deployment or failed deployment;
- workflows being redesigned;
- businesses paying for solutions;
- measurable productivity or operational effects;
- repeated customer problems;
- small-business automation;
- labour-market or business-model changes.

Add a business angle only when the evidence supports one. Do not manufacture an opportunity.

Include important non-AI technology only when it has significant economic, security, infrastructure, platform, semiconductor, or regulatory consequences.
