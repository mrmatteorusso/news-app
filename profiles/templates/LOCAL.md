# Local intelligence profile

Local importance is different from global importance. A road closure can outrank a national political statement when it saves the reader time, money, risk, or surprise.

Prioritise actionable developments involving roads, tunnels, public transport, healthcare, hospitals, municipal procedures, taxes or fees, schools, public works, utilities, environmental restrictions, Civil Protection, and major events that disrupt services or traffic.

Prefer official local sources. A public Facebook post from the responsible official body can be a primary source. Unofficial community posts are discovery signals and need corroboration.

Avoid village-event volume, routine publicity, and ceremonial announcements.
