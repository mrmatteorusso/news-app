# Crypto news profile

Prioritise consequential developments involving Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Cardano/ADA. Also include genuinely important industry-wide regulation, security, custody, stablecoin, exchange, infrastructure, institutional-access, and adoption developments.

Select items when something materially changed. Examples include major protocol upgrades, confirmed governance or treasury decisions, severe exploits, systemic exchange or custody failures, significant court or regulatory decisions, important institutional-access changes, or adoption with credible evidence.

Exclude routine price predictions, influencer commentary, token promotion, minor partnerships, ordinary volatility, unverified rumours, technical proposals that have not progressed, and repetitive market commentary.

Price movements belong in the market dashboard. Crypto News should explain the important event behind a movement when one exists.

Signals from X, Reddit, or community forums require corroboration through primary documentation or reputable reporting before being presented as fact.
